Picture Book Word Count: How Many Words Should a Picture Book Be? (Ages 3-7)

Picture books are 0-500 words, 32 pages. Under 500 words for traditional publishers. Checklist included - gets backlinks from writing blogs. • Built in Austin, Texas, USA

The 500-Word Rule Publishers Use

Traditional publishers reject picture books over 500 words. Why? 32 pages, illustration-driven, read-aloud in 5 minutes.

  • 0-500 words: Picture Book (32 pages, Ages 3-7, 1-2 sentences per page)
  • 500-2500: Early Reader (64 pages, Ages 5-7, repetition)
  • Ideal: 300-400 words, hook on page 1, twist on page 28

Picture Book Checklist (Linkable Asset)

  • ✅ Under 500 words?
  • ✅ 32 pages standard?
  • ✅ Hook on page 1-2?
  • ✅ Illustration note every page?
  • ✅ Read-aloud under 5 min?
