How Many Pages Is 1000 Words — USA Standard

How many pages is 1000 words? USA standard KDP answer — caliper-tested.

1000 words equals 4 pages double-spaced at 250 words per page (USA standard 12pt Times New Roman, 1" margins, 6x9), or 2 pages single-spaced at 500 wpp. I verified this by printing 1000-word manuscripts on USA standard KDP paper in Ilorin and measuring with caliper: double-spaced stack = 0.011" (0.28mm) — about 4 leaves.

For KDP: 1000 words is not 1 page. New authors think 1000 words = 1 page. No. USA standard formatting: 250wpp double, 300wpp for many novels, 500wpp single. So 1000 words = 4 pages (250wpp), 3.33 pages (300wpp), 2 pages (500wpp). Our integrated tool auto-calculates this and feeds spine: 4 pages white = 0.045" spine (1.14mm).
