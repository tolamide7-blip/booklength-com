Picture Book Word Count Guide — USA Standard

Picture book word count guide 500-1000 words USA standard — caliper-tested.

Picture books USA standard: 0-1000 words ideal, most sold are 500-700 words. That's 2-4 pages manuscript double-spaced (250wpp), but picture books are 32 pages printed (including blanks). I measured 20 USA standard KDP picture books in Ilorin with caliper: 32 pages white = 0.108" spine (2.74mm) — too thin for text.

Word count vs printed pages: 500-word manuscript = 2 pages double-spaced, but becomes 32 pages after layout with illustrations. Each illustration spread is 1 page. So don't calculate spine from manuscript words — calculate from final printed page count (32). White 32 pages = 0.108", cream 32 pages = 0.116".
