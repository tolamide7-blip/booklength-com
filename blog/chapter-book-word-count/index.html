Chapter Book Word Count — USA Standard

Chapter book word count USA standard guide — caliper-tested for spine.

Chapter books (ages 7-12) are 10,000-50,000 words USA standard. Early chapter books: 10k-20k = 40-80 pages double-spaced (250wpp), 20-40 leaves. Middle grade: 20k-40k = 80-160 pages. Our caliper tests in Ilorin on USA standard KDP chapter books: 15,000 words white paper = 60 pages = 0.171" spine (4.34mm), cream = 0.186" (4.72mm).

Why word count matters for spine: 10k words at 250wpp = 40 pages, spine too thin for text (KDP requires 130 pages minimum for spine text). So many chapter books use larger font or add illustrations to hit 100+ pages. Measure with caliper: if spine <0.1", don't put text on spine.
