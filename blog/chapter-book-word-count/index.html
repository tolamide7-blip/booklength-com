How Long Should a Chapter Book Be? (Chapter Book Word Count by Age)

Chapter books 4000-15000 words, Middle Grade 20000-55000, YA 50000-90000. Age chart for backlinks. • Built in Austin, Texas, USA

Word Count by Age (The Chart Agents Share)

  • Chapter Book (Ages 6-9): 4000-15000 words, 80-120 pages, illustrations each chapter
  • Middle Grade (8-12): 20000-55000 words, 200-300 pages, adventure
  • Young Adult (12-18): 50000-90000 words, 250-400 pages, coming-of-age
  • New Adult (18-30): 70000-100000 words

Can I query agents? Chapter Book under 15000, MG under 55000, YA under 90000 = yes.
