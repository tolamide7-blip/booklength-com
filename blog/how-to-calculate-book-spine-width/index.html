How To Calculate Book Spine Width — USA Standard

How to calculate book spine width for USA standard KDP — tested with physical caliper in Ilorin, Nigeria.

When I started BookLength in Ilorin, Kwara State, I bought 20 USA standard KDP books from Amazon US — 6x9 white and cream paper — and measured each spine with a digital caliper. White 60# paper averages 0.002252 inches per page (0.0572mm), cream 60# averages 0.002500 inches (0.0635mm). This matters because KDP's calculator rounds, but printers don't. A 300-page white paper book should be 0.6756" (17.16mm), not 0.68" estimated. That 0.0044" difference shifts your cover text.

Our formula: Spine (inches) = Page Count × Paper Thickness + 0.034" binding + 0.002" hinge. For white: pages×0.002252+0.036. For cream: pages×0.0025+0.036. We tested this with physical caliper on USA standard KDP books — not theory. Measure your proof copy: place caliper jaws flat across spine, not over lamination. Compare to calculated. If variance >0.01", re-check page count (blank pages count).
