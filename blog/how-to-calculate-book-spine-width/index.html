How to Calculate Book Spine Width (Ultimate Guide for KDP and Ingram)

Exact formula Pages × Paper Thickness = Spine. Includes White vs Cream paper thickness for KDP and Ingram UK. Copy-paste formula for backlinks. • Built in Austin, Texas, USA

The Formula Everyone Links To

Spine Width = Page Count × Paper Thickness

  • White 50lb (White): 0.002252" (0.0572mm) - most common for KDP
  • Cream 50lb: 0.0025" (0.0635mm) - premium for novels
  • White 70lb: 0.0025" - color interiors
  • Cream 60lb: 0.0028" - luxury

Example: 250 pages × 0.002252" = 0.563" (14.30mm)

KDP vs Ingram UK: Same Thickness, Different Units

Same paper thickness works in US and UK. KDP wants inches, Ingram UK wants mm. Our calculator toggles instantly: 0.563" = 14.3mm. Cover wrap = (Trim Width ×2) + Spine + 0.25" bleed.
